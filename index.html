Skills

Three things I do repeatedly and well. Each one shows up in every project below, usually in the same order.

01

CAD — parametric + PCB

Fully parametric assemblies so a changed wheel diameter or gear ratio propagates through the whole model, plus board layout for the electronics that live inside them.

02

3D printing

Designing for the process rather than around it: print orientation, wall and infill choices for load paths, and tolerances tuned per printer so parts fit off the bed.

03

Parts assembly

Building, wiring, and tuning the finished machine — bearing preload, belt tension, fastener access — and rebuilding the parts that fail during testing.
