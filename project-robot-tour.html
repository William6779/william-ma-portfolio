Robot Tour

A Raspberry Pi-controlled tour robot that synchronizes stepper motors for smooth, repeatable movement through the course.

Build overview

The control problem was less about making the motors move and more about making them move together. Coordinated ramps reduce jerk at corners and make the robot easier to tune for a repeatable course run.
